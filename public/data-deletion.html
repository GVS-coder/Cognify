Data Deletion Instructions

Delete Individual Data Without Deleting Your Account

Cognify allows users to delete individual items without deleting their Cognify account. You can remove tasks, reminders, and knowledge-vault entries directly within the app.

How to Delete Your Data

  1. Open the Cognify app on your device.
  2. Sign in to your Cognify account.
  3. Navigate to the relevant section: Tasks, Reminders, or Knowledge Vault.
  4. Open or select the item you want to remove.
  5. Use the delete or trash option and confirm the deletion when prompted.

Repeat these steps for each item you want to delete. Your account remains active when you delete individual items.

What Data Can You Delete?

  • Individual tasks
  • Individual reminders
  • Individual knowledge-vault entries

Delete each item through the corresponding feature in the app.

What Happens to Deleted Data?

Deleted items are intended to be removed from the data managed by Cognify for those items. Any copies or records retained by service providers, backups, or logs, if applicable, are subject to their applicable retention practices and Cognify's privacy policy.

Cognify does not promise a specific additional retention period for backups or service-provider records on this page.
